# PhD advisor shortlist: analog / mixed-signal IC design

Compiled 2026-10-29 for fall 2027 entry (December 2026 deadlines). Degree target: EE/ECE.

Goal of the PhD: five years of transistor-level analog and mixed-signal circuit design practice under an advisor who tapes out and measures silicon every year.

Filter applied to every name: tenure-track in the EE department, students tape out and measure analog, mixed-signal or RF CMOS as the core of the PhD, plausibly taking students for fall 2027. Every entry was checked against the school's official faculty directory and against chip papers from 2022 to 2026 (ISSCC, JSSC, VLSI Symposium, CICC, RFIC). Names that are now emeritus, adjunct, in industry or at another school were removed.

## What this says about the school ranking

Berkeley, Columbia and MIT each have three genuine groups, so a first-choice refusal still leaves a home. Caltech has two superb groups and nothing behind them. Stanford has two strong groups plus Poon. Harvard and Yale each have exactly one, so the PhD depends on a single person saying yes. For Harvard the application is in and the relationship exists. For Yale, email Natarajan directly before paying the application fee.

## Hooks for the statement of purpose

- Kinget is the purest analog apprenticeship on the list: ADCs, sensor readout ICs, RF front-ends, a steady group of five or six, and a 49-year Columbia analog lineage through Tsividis.
- Shepard, Muller, Emami and Sideris are where the wireless headstage story lands directly: Shepard's 65,536-electrode CMOS array, Muller's neural front-ends, Emami's brain-machine-interface chips, Sideris' neural stimulator.
- Han, Niknejad, Hajimiri and Natarajan are the mm-wave and RF powerhouses. Not the thesis topic, but the heaviest tape-out cadence anywhere, and Han's 2026 graduate went to Neuralink.
- Lee is the one person on the list whose entire career is data converters, which is the circuit the thesis problem reduces to.

## Per-school detail

### Harvard (application submitted)

- **Donhee Ham**, John A. and Elizabeth S. Armstrong Professor of Engineering and Applied Sciences, listed under Applied Physics. Lab: donheehamlab.org. 4,096-channel current-clamp CMOS neural interface (2026), parallel intracellular recording of thousands of neurons (Nature Biomedical Engineering 2025), CMOS NMR spectrometer (JSSC 2024, VLSI 2023), ISSCC 2022 image sensor. Group: 7 postdocs, 4 PhD students. Judgment HIGH, but project assignment can land on the biology side. Resigned a Samsung SAIT vice-presidency in January 2025, active at Harvard through 2026.
- **Gage Hills**, Associate Professor of Electrical Engineering, joined 2021. Lab: nanodesign.seas.harvard.edu. DAC 2025 monolithic 3D eDRAM, VLSI 2025 digital-to-optical converters, VLSI 2023 CNFET+RRAM stack, Nature 2024 bendable RISC-V (co-author). About 7 PhD students. Analog and RF content exists only in the electronic-photonic transceiver thread. Judgment MEDIUM.
- **Gu-Yeon Wei**, Robert and Suzanne Case Professor of EE and CS, with David Brooks as co-PI. Lab: vlsiarch.eecs.harvard.edu. CICC 2026 16 nm training accelerator, JSSC 2025 EPOCHS-1 12 nm SoC with integrated voltage regulation, VLSI 2024 hybrid DVFS. Yearly advanced-node tape-outs, but mixed-signal is the power-management slice. Judgment MEDIUM.
- Excluded: Hongkun Park (on leave as Samsung SAIT president from January 2026), Woodward Yang (no output since 2010), Jia Liu (no CMOS), Kiyoul Yang (photonics). No circuits hires 2023 to 2026.

### MIT

- **Ruonan Han**, Professor, director of CICS. Lab: hangroup.mit.edu. Three ISSCC 2026 papers (sub-THz transceiver, cryo-CMOS quantum controller, photovoltaic PUF), Nature Electronics 2025, ISSCC 2025, VLSI 2025 and JSSC 2026 fractional-N PLL. 11 PhD students, 3 graduated in 2026 (one to Neuralink). Judgment HIGH.
- **Negar Reiskarimian**, Associate Professor without tenure since July 2025. Lab: radiuslab.mit.edu. ISSCC 2023, ISSCC 2024 (Jack Kilby Outstanding Student Paper), RFIC 2024 (best student paper), RFIC 2025, JSSC extensions 2023, 2024, 2026. 4 PhD students, first graduate 2025 to Apple. Judgment HIGH.
- **Hae-Seung Lee**, ATSP Professor. Lab: hslee.mit.edu. JSSC 2023 6.4 GS/s continuous-time pipelined ADC in 16 nm with ADI, zero-crossing ADC work ongoing, no ISSCC, VLSI or CICC paper found 2024 to 2026. Group appears to be 1 to 3 students. Also chief technologist of Omni Design. Judgment HIGH per student, LOW to MEDIUM capacity. Ask directly.
- Alternate: **Anantha Chandrakasan**, provost since July 2025, group still tapes out bio-chips (VLSI 2026 GMINI neural interface chip, CICC 2026 ingestible stimulator). Co-advising arrangement expected.
- Excluded: Sodini (post-tenure, no ICs since 2022), Sze and Song Han (digital), Perreault, Lang and Coday (power electronics, no ICs), Shulaker (left MIT). No circuits hires 2023 to 2026.

### Columbia

- **Peter Kinget**, Bernard J. Lechner Professor of Electrical Engineering. Lab: kingetgroup.ee.columbia.edu. ISSCC 2022 and JSSC 2023 phase interpolator, VLSI 2022 and JSSC 2023 analog feature extractor, CICC 2024 beamforming receiver, RFIC 2025 analog matched filter, OJ-SSCS 2025 and 2026 radiation-hard ADCs, JSSC 2026 sensor-interface readout IC. 5 to 6 PhD students, 22 alumni at ADI, Intel, Apple, Qualcomm. Judgment HIGH.
- **Ken Shepard**, Lau Family Professor of Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering and Neurological Sciences. Lab: bioee.ee.columbia.edu. VLSI 2023 and Nature Electronics 2025 wireless 65,536-electrode subdural array with 1,024 simultaneous channels, Nature Electronics 2023 to 2025 OLED-on-CMOS optogenetic probes and implantable imagers, CICC 2024 ultrasound front-end. 17 PhD students, 6 research scientists. Circuit share depends on the project. Judgment HIGH on chip projects.
- **Mingoo Seok**, Associate Professor, chair of the Computer Engineering program. ISSCC 2022 to 2025 every year (in-memory computing, charge-domain GPS accelerator, probing-attack detector, 16 nm transformer accelerator), VLSI 2025, CICC 2025 image sensor with analog DCT and GaN DC-DC converter, JSSC 2024 digital LDO. About 6 PhD students, graduates at Apple, Intel, TSMC, Google. Judgment MEDIUM to HIGH, leaning power management and compute-in-memory.
- Alternate: **Harish Krishnaswamy**, Professor. ISSCC 2023 and 2026, JSSC 2023 and 2024, RFIC 2024 and 2025, cryo-CMOS and GaN work. Managing director of Sivers Wireless since 2022, chief strategy officer since September 2026. Availability unverified.
- Excluded: Tsividis (49-year retrospective lecture October 2025, no independent output), Preindl (board-level power electronics, in France through late 2026), Khodagholy (moved to UC Irvine 2024), Eisner (GaN devices), Zukowski (inactive). An ICS-emphasis faculty search for July 2026 was posted; outcome unknown.

### Yale

ISSCC 2023, 2024 and 2025, JSSC 2023, 2024 and 2025, CICC 2026, RF correlators and N-path receivers in 22 nm and 45 nm. 5 PhD students transplanted from OSU, 12 PhD alumni at Qualcomm, Apple, Intel.
